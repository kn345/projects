The Shared Socioeconomic Pathways and their energy, land use, and greenhouse gas emissions implications
2016
Global Environmental Change
Open Access
Abstract
This overview examines the Shared Socioeconomic Pathways (SSPs) developed for the IPCC 6th Assessment Report—narrative scenarios describing how society, demographics, and technology may develop over the 21st century, with differing challenges to climate change mitigation and adaptation. We assess energy use, land use, and greenhouse gas emissions across five distinct pathways ranging from sustainability-focused to fossil-fuel intensive development.

The Shared Socioeconomic Pathways and their energy, land use, and greenhouse gas emissions implications
2016
Global Environmental Change
Open Access
Summary
Plain language
Scientists mapped out five possible futures for how the world might develop by 2100—from a clean-energy, sustainable path to one heavily dependent on fossil fuels. The study shows how each future would affect global energy use, land, and the amount of carbon dioxide released into the atmosphere, helping policymakers prepare for different scenarios.
